    How It Works

    Small safety signals, one readable plan.

    01

    Heat load

    Temperature, humidity, coat, age, and face shape raise or lower the caution score.

    02

    Paw risk

    Pavement and walk length adjust the plan toward shade, shorter loops, or indoor games.

    03

    Backup joy

    If the walk is risky, PawPace suggests enrichment instead of simply saying no.

    04

    Real numbers

    Open-Meteo can ground the planner with local temperature, humidity, and UV.
