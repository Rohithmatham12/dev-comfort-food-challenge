Monsoon Rasam Window

A rainy-evening comfort bowl drawn with semantic HTML and CSS: no canvas, no SVG, no image art.

CSS art of rasam rice by a rainy window

A warm bowl of rasam rice sits on a table with curry leaves, pepper, papad, a small pickle cup, drifting steam, a brass lamp, and a rain-speckled window behind it.

rain outside · pepper steam inside

How it is drawn

  • Pure HTML and CSS shapes, gradients, shadows, and pseudo-depth.
  • CSS-only rain, steam, lamp flame, and rasam shimmer animations.
  • Accessible fallback text through a labelled artwork region.
  • Reduced-motion support for visitors who prefer less animation.
